# DemoShop damaged items and customer support
Fictional training policy, version 2026-10-26. Applies only to the DemoShop example.

## Damaged, defective, or incorrect items
Report a damaged, defective, or incorrect item within 48 hours of delivery through My Orders. Upload photographs of the item, packaging, and shipping label through the secure form. Keep the packaging until the case is resolved. Support reviews the report within 2 business days. Approved cases qualify for a free replacement if stock is available, or a refund. Pickup is free. The 48-hour reporting rule applies even to categories excluded from change-of-mind returns.

## Contact and scope
Demo support email is support@demoshop.example. Support operates Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time, excluding public holidays. This is a fictional address, not a real support service. The assistant can explain published policies only; it cannot inspect accounts, confirm payment status, promise case approval, or perform order actions.

# DemoShop shipping policy
Fictional training policy, version 2026-10-26. Applies only to the DemoShop example.

## Delivery time and coverage
DemoShop ships within India only. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch in eligible postal codes. Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Processing before dispatch takes up to 1 business day.

## Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free for an order subtotal of INR 999 or more after discounts. Below INR 999, standard shipping costs INR 79. Express shipping costs INR 149 regardless of order value. Availability is shown at checkout.

## Tracking and delays
A tracking link is emailed after dispatch. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours. If delivery is more than 2 business days past its estimated date, contact support with the order number. This knowledge assistant cannot access live tracking or customer orders.

# DemoShop cancellations and changes
Fictional training policy, version 2026-10-26. Applies only to the DemoShop example.

## Before dispatch
An order may be cancelled from My Orders before dispatch, without a cancellation fee. The cancellation refund is initiated within 1 business day, then may take 5 to 7 business days to reach the original payment method. Unpaid cash-on-delivery cancellations require no refund.

## After dispatch
An order cannot be cancelled after dispatch. The customer may request an eligible return after delivery under the returns policy. This chat assistant explains policies but cannot cancel an order or submit a return.

## Address changes
A delivery address may be changed through My Orders only before the order is packed. Once packed, contact support; a change is not guaranteed. Changes are not supported after dispatch. Product size or color cannot be edited on an existing order; cancel before dispatch and place a new order.

# DemoShop refunds policy
Fictional training policy, version 2026-10-26. Applies only to the DemoShop example.

## Inspection and refund timing
A returned item is inspected within 2 business days after reaching the warehouse. If approved, the refund is initiated within 1 business day. Banks or payment providers may then take 5 to 7 business days to credit the money. These periods start at different stages and are not counted from the pickup date.

## Refund destination and amount
Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method. Cash-on-delivery refunds are made to the bank account supplied through the secure return form. Never share bank details, passwords, or OTPs in chat. Refunds cover the eligible item amount actually paid, less any applicable extra pickup fee. Original shipping charges are not refunded for change-of-mind returns. Original shipping charges are refunded when the entire order was damaged or incorrect.
